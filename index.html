How a hand plays

  1. 一

    Open the gate

    Two cards are dealt as the posts. A counts 1, K counts 13.

  2. 二

    Size your shot

    Bet up to your stack or the pot, whichever is smaller. Wide gates earn bold bets.

  3. 三

    Shoot

    Between the posts wins from the pot. Outside loses the bet. Matching a post costs double.
